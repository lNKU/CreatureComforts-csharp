# CreatureComforts-csharp
### About
---
**_CreatureComforts_** is my take on a small quality-of-life and mild progression rebalance that tweaks drain rates, adjusts carrying capacity, gets rid of some nonsensical (to me) inventory restrictions, and tweaks hideout build and craft timers. If you like being a loot goblin, this mod will be sure to whet your hoarding addiction!

The mod may feel a little unfocused, but I adjusted what I felt were some annoyingly rough edges with the game.

Some features can be adjusted via a generated config.json file that will generate on initial load.

---

### Quick Highlights 
- Progression & Economy: Configurable Flea Market level requirement (default: Level 25), shorter, randomized Scav cooldowns (default: 5–15 minutes) down from the original 30 minutes. As well as an exponential experience curve to extend the early game out more.

- Character & Movement: Halves passive energy and hydration drain. Also increases weight limits by roughly 20-30ish percent so you can hold more stuff before you feel like the beached whale you're destined to become!

- Weapons & Gear: Removes the nonsensical weapon folding restrictions that some attachments have. Additionally, gets rid of the asinine extra durability degradation from using suppressors and powerful/high-tier ammunition. If the bots get to be OP, so do you dammit!

- Hideout & Logistics: Increases the capacity for Metal and Expeditionary fuel tanks to 220 and 100 units, respectively, allowing for you to run your generator longer before needing to refuel. Sets Bitcoin craft time to 3.5 hours, and randomizes area upgrade and general item crafting timers. I wanted to have _some_ wait while building my hideout, but I didn't want instant building/crafting timers, although these are adjustable.
